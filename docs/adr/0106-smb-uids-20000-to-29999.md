# ADR-0106: SMB accounts use uids 20000-29999 and gid 20000

## Context

Superseded by ADR-0107: SMB accounts use uids 70000-79999 and gid 70000.

SMB host accounts (ADR-0105) need explicit uids that UNOS allocates, stores and never hands out twice, and their primary group unos-smb needs a fixed gid, because both end up in the ownership of files on the pools.

## Decision

The first design of slice 2 gave SMB accounts explicit uids from 20000 to 29999 and the primary group unos-smb the gid 20000. No reason for the range was recorded.

## Consequences

- The range lies below `UID_MAX` and `GID_MAX` of the host's login.defs (60000), where a hand-made `useradd` or `groupadd` can land: such an account could take the uid of a deleted UNOS user and with it that user's files. ADR-0107 moves the range above those limits.

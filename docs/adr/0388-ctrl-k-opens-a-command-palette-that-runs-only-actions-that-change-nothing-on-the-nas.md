# ADR-0388: Ctrl+K opens a command palette of the pages and actions of the user's role, which runs only actions that change nothing on the NAS

## Context

The feature `webui.command-palette` asks that Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on macOS) open a command palette that goes to any page or action: typing narrows the choices, and Enter opens the chosen page or starts the action. The owner delegated its decisions to the agent, asking above all that it be safe (ADR-0013): what it offers to each role, which actions it may run itself, how it matches, its keyboard and the browser's own Ctrl+K, the phone, and that it asks the server for nothing.

## Decision

- Where: `webui/src/lib/components/palette/CommandPalette.svelte`, which `webui/src/routes/+layout.svelte` mounts on a signed-in page that the user may see, once the account is loaded, and not for an account that can only set up two-factor authentication (ADR-0386). The sign-in, first-run and two-factor setup pages of such an account have no palette, and Ctrl+K stays the browser's there.
- What it offers, from the role that the layout has already loaded (ADR-0030): an administrator gets every page of the sidebar in its order, with the Trash of Files after Files, every tab of System (`/system#<tab>`), and the actions; a regular user gets the pages of a regular user's sidebar, Dashboard and Account (`/system#security`), and the two actions every user has. The names are those of the sidebar and of System's tabs (the `layout`, `system` and `files` catalogs); the actions and the other words a page is known by (keywords such as SMART for Disk Health) are in `webui/src/lib/i18n/catalog/palette.ts`. Each option shows its name in the page's language and its kind (Page, System or Action). The server's roles stay the guard: the layout and the API refuse a page or a request that the palette would not offer.
- Actions it runs itself, since they change nothing on the NAS and are undone at once: the language of this browser (the one the page is not in, kept as the Account page keeps it, ADR-0287), and Logout, as the sidebar's Logout does it. Every action that changes the NAS opens its page's own dialog instead, which asks and acts only when the user confirms it there: "Add a user…" opens the Add user dialog of the Users page, and "Reboot the host…", "Shut down the host…" and "Suspend the host…" open the confirmations of System > Power (ADR-0233). Their names end with an ellipsis. The palette asks through the store `paletteRequest` of `webui/src/lib/stores/palette.ts`, `{ dialog, at }`: the page of `at` (`webui/src/routes/users/+page.svelte`, `webui/src/routes/system/tabs/PowerTab.svelte`) takes it, sets it to null and opens its dialog; the palette drops a request that no page took as soon as the browser is elsewhere than `at` (`afterNavigate`), so that a dialog never opens later by surprise. A store and not the URL, so that no link can open a confirmation. No other destructive action (deleting, emptying the trash, a restore, an unmount) is offered: the palette goes to its page.
- Matching (`webui/src/lib/components/palette/match.ts`): a choice is found by its names in every language of the web UI, the page's and the other (`translateIn` of `webui/src/lib/i18n/index.ts`), and by its keywords in both. Texts are compared in lower case, with the Russian letter yo as ye and single spaces. The best first: a name that starts with the query; a name whose words start with each word of the query; a name that holds the query; each word of the query starting a word of the names or keywords; each word held by them. Equals keep the order of the list, and an empty field lists every choice.
- Keyboard: Ctrl+K or Cmd+K without Shift or Alt, on every system, by the letter K of the layout or, in a layout without Latin letters (Russian), by the key K. It opens the palette also from a text field; on macOS Ctrl+K in a text field stays the field's own (it deletes to the end of the line). The field is a combobox (`Input` with `listbox`: `role="combobox"`, `aria-controls`, `aria-activedescendant`) over a listbox of options, and the focus stays in the field: ↓ and ↑ move the choice, round the ends of the list, typing chooses the first option again, and Enter or a click opens the chosen one. The palette is a `Modal` on the stack of ADR-0361: Escape closes only it, and its close button, a click beside it and a second Ctrl+K close it too. Opened from the menu drawer of a phone, it covers the drawer, which stays open under it: `+layout.svelte` leaves Escape to an open dialog (`aria-modal`), whose listener can come after the menu's, a click inside such a dialog is not a click beside the drawer, and the focus that moves into it does not count as leaving the drawer (ADR-0302). Tab and Shift+Tab go round its field and its close button, a trap of its own (Modal has none). When it closes, the focus goes back where it was, before the chosen page opens or the action runs. A screen reader hears how many choices are left (a `role="status"`).
- The browser's own Ctrl+K (the search of the address bar in Chrome and Edge, of the search bar in Firefox): UNOS takes the key (`preventDefault`) only when it opens or closes the palette. The palette never opens over a dialog, which the user finishes or cancels first: choosing a page from it would leave a dialog's unsaved form, or a busy dialog that may not be closed, behind. A dialog is a Modal or one written by hand with `aria-modal="true"` or the class `modal-overlay`; the two dialogs of System > Scheduler take `modal-overlay` in place of the same utility classes. While one is open, and on the pages without the palette, Ctrl+K is the browser's.
- The phone: below Tailwind's md breakpoint a button of the header bar, "Open the command palette", opens it. It comes after the drawer in the DOM and sits at the bar's right end, so that the links still follow the menu button in the tab order (ADR-0302); it closes the drawer first. From md up there is no such button, and the sidebar's first button is still Logout. The hint of the keys shows from md up only.
- It asks the server for nothing: its choices come from the account the layout loaded and from the catalogs, its markup exists only while it is open, and it has no timer (ADR-0365). What a chosen page loads, and Logout's request, are theirs.
- Tests: `webui/tests/command-palette.spec.ts` (every page of the sidebar and tab of System and the actions of an administrator, the narrowing in both languages, no request while it opens, narrows and closes, Enter, the arrows and a click, the language and Logout, the power confirmations and the Add user dialog with nothing sent, Escape over the open menu, the focus given back, Tab kept inside, Ctrl+K in a text field and on macOS, not over a dialog, not on the sign-in page nor for a setup-only account, a regular user's four choices, the phone's button), `webui/tests/mobile-layout.spec.ts` (the palette at 375 x 812 in both languages and at 667 x 375; no button from md up) and `supervisor/tests/test_command_palette.py` (the ranks of `match.ts`, run with node).

## Consequences

- A page added to the sidebar or a tab added to System must be added to the palette's lists: the first test of the spec compares them and fails until it is.
- A new action of the palette that changes the NAS can only be a request to its page's own dialog: a value of `PaletteDialog` and a page that takes it.
- On a signed-in page Ctrl+K no longer reaches the browser's search; the browser keeps Ctrl+L, Ctrl+E and Alt+D for its address bar.
- The palette finds pages and actions, not the NAS's data (a share, a container, a user by name): that would ask the server.

## Considered options

- Running Reboot or Shut down from the palette after a confirmation of its own: a second dialog to keep in step with System > Power's, which also shows a pending reboot and its Cancel.
- A URL that opens a page's dialog (`/system?confirm=reboot#power`): any link could then open a confirmation.
- Opening the palette over a dialog: see above.
- No way to open it on a phone: touch screens would then have only the menu, without the actions.
